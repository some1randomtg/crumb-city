Click the town to steer with your mouse; Esc frees the cursor. Flying drains your wing meter, so land anywhere (rooftops count) to refill it. Hold S in the air to slow down and use the ring under you to line up a landing. Townsfolk with a teal marker have a favour to ask: walk up and press E to chat. A few shops have their doors open, too. Careful: plenty of people don't like pigeons. Watch for the red "!" and fly off before they kick. The tram stops at seven shelters around the park: hop on when the doors open for a free ride. Roost Court, the brick apartment block east of the park, is open floor to floor: take the stairs, or find an open window. Traffic stops at red lights, and drivers will brake and honk if you sit in the road. Beyond the old centre lie Old Town, the Harbour, the Market Quarter, the West End and Sparrow Hill. Under the streets, the Crumb Line links four stations: look for the yellow ring signs and the stairs going down. The River Quill runs along the south of the city, with a Riverbus between three piers, and the coast road leads to the beach at Gull Bay. Stand on any boat and it carries you along. Some of the people you pass have names and plenty to say: press E to chat. Featherstone Zoo is in the far north-west. Tap or click the minimap (or press N) for a bigger map, then tap it again to drop a waypoint. North of Old Town, Featherstone Central station has steam trains to Westgate and Eastgate. And down by the pond, a lilac-necked pigeon called Plume might just catch your eye. Every couple of minutes something happens in town (a parade, a robbery, a wedding...): watch the purple star on the map and the card under your quest. Streets have names now, too. Hop off the train at the new Featherland halt for a theme park: ride everything, hug Captain Coo, and stay for the fireworks. Land on someone's head for a ride (and peck their hat off), peck the street furniture, play the floor piano on the Market Quarter plaza, and try the playground in the Harbour green. Glints on rooftops and in bins are loot: open your Bag (I) to look at it, use it, and read the clues you've found. Four new shops have puzzles to solve, and the wind now blows: ride the swirling-leaf updrafts. Open the Map (N, or tap the minimap) to set waypoints and fly back to places you've visited, and the Journal (J) has tabs for favours, places, stats and a log.
