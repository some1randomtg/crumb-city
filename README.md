# The Pecking Order

A cosy 3D game about a city pigeon, in one HTML file (formerly Crumb City). Play it at https://some1randomtg.github.io/crumb-city/

Fly across seven cities and a little bit of the moon, make friends, run errands and find out who really runs the pigeons.

Works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Browsers with WebGPU get the new renderer; others keep the classic look. Progress is saved in your browser.

Current version: v355 (the bare walls all over the world dressed with character, from the Arc de Triomphe's reliefs to Tokyoo's back alleys, and Bangcluck's traffic taking turns at the junctions; on top of everything since v344.1: a second look at every city by a new set of automatic visual checks, 431 signs with a character of their own, lit windows with people in them, a faster Paree, and 486 small signs of life: cats on counters, steaming kettles, swinging signs and a few hundred new things to find)
