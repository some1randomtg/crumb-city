# The Pecking Order

A cosy 3D game about a city pigeon, in one HTML file (formerly Crumb City). Play it at https://some1randomtg.github.io/crumb-city/

Fly across seven cities and a little bit of the moon, make friends, run errands and find out who really runs the pigeons.

Works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Browsers with WebGPU get the new renderer; others keep the classic look. Progress is saved in your browser.

Current version: v504 (bon voyage: getting between cities, every flight into New Yolk landing its own jet, a glass front and new jet bridges at New Yolk International, the Euro Express past the vineyards and a Brussels halt with Pidge on the roof, a real Libirdy ferry crossing, and a little map of your trip while you travel; before that v503.1, clear view: Family Ties tidied, the cannoli box on Mama Pica's counter, a clear look at the sleeping Admiral with Gary's gaze in wider dashes, and Carmine taking Mama's call when the cannoli go soft; and v503, rising sun: Tokyoo's first and last impressions, Air Tokyoo's full cabin with a kid waving over her seat, fans with signs at Haneeda, Kyotoo's temples and Okimi-san's rationed matcha, Dotonborii's signs and Myaku-Pecku, Naraa's deer, snow monkeys with Punch, and a matsuri with dancers, a drummer and fireworks)
