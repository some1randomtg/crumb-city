# The Pecking Order

A cosy 3D game about a city pigeon, in one HTML file (formerly Crumb City). Play it at https://some1randomtg.github.io/crumb-city/

Fly across seven cities and a little bit of the moon, make friends, run errands and find out who really runs the pigeons.

Works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Browsers with WebGPU get the new renderer; others keep the classic look. Progress is saved in your browser.

Current version: v448 (on one leg: meet Odette "Stumpy" Hopwood, born with one leg in Little Pecking, who dances the swan in all eight places, from busking in Times Squab to opening night at the Opéra and the first ballet on the Moon; before that v447, finders keepers: everything Pidge finds on the ground is the thing its name says, from a crumb that is a crumb and each city's own food to 25 shinies that were all one disc, and food hops into Pidge's bill when eaten; before that v446, odd birds: every bird that isn't a pigeon is its own kind now, from dumpling gulls and the Magpie Mob to Bangcluck's hens in eight breeds and Coco the cockatoo)..
