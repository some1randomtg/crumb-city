# The Pecking Order

A cosy 3D game about a city pigeon, in one HTML file (formerly Crumb City). Play it at https://some1randomtg.github.io/crumb-city/

Fly across seven cities and a little bit of the moon, make friends, run errands and find out who really runs the pigeons.

Works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Browsers with WebGPU get the new renderer; others keep the classic look. Progress is saved in your browser.

Current version: v435 (fun house, v435: a haunted house in Featherland, five minutes in the dark with Honkers the clown, with places to hide, doors to slam and pigeon holes he can't fit through, and his Red Nose for anyone who lasts; then soft landing, v433: Pidge lands where she means to, on lamps, bollards, chimneys and statue heads, with a ring that shows where she will touch down; the compass says where an objective is, a gold marker pulses at the door and a search shows its area; toasts wait for a conversation to end; a failed mission offers Try again from a checkpoint with what went wrong; stealth gear counts and the first jobs can be done quietly; after time away a card recalls where the story stands; Grandpa Coo, Old Gus, Mama Pica and Paulie remember what you've done; each gear set's third piece changes how you play; and the Auto graphics setting keeps a step only when it makes the game faster)
