# The Pecking Order

A cosy 3D game about a city pigeon, in one HTML file (formerly Crumb City). Play it at https://some1randomtg.github.io/crumb-city/

Fly across seven cities and a little bit of the moon, make friends, run errands and find out who really runs the pigeons.

Works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Browsers with WebGPU get the new renderer; others keep the classic look. Progress is saved in your browser.

Current version: v356 (nearly two thousand small touches all over the world: the Broodlyn Bridge's lights after dark, Hachicoo turning his head to watch you, the Tower's lift climbing its leg, frost at dawn in St. Petersbird, Earth's thunderstorms from the moon and a flock that lies down for the night; on top of v355: Bangcluck's traffic taking turns, the bare walls dressed with character, and everything since v344.1)
