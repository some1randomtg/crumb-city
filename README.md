# The Pecking Order

A cosy 3D game about a city pigeon, in one HTML file (formerly Crumb City). Play it at https://some1randomtg.github.io/crumb-city/

Fly across seven cities and a little bit of the moon, make friends, run errands and find out who really runs the pigeons.

Works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Browsers with WebGPU get the new renderer; others keep the classic look. Progress is saved in your browser.

Current version: v429 (sleeping with the fishes, v429: New Yolk's waterfront polished, with the Statue of Libirdy sculpted, the Broodlyn Bridge on stone arches and the train to Eastgate on a pier instead of over the water; the Aquarium inside and out, whale, sea lions and shark tank; the Don wants Boris "sleeping with the fishes", and Pidge tucks him in; Ksenia Volkova joins the Countess in St. Petersbird; and a courtroom quest where Pidge defends Rodion, who thinks he is Napoleon, with seven verdicts; then breaking bread, v428: a new job for the Don before Bangcluck, a Breaking Bad parody in New Yolk: a goose lawyer on Route 66, two fennecs cooking blue rock candy in an RV at the end of Maple Grove, a police chase you drive into the city, and a ramp off Pier 7 into the belly of a blimp; then swan song, v427: King Ludwig's ball in Bavaria is a cinematic now, in by clockwork swan through his grotto, a waltz with Pidge among the mannequins to Lohengrin on a music box, Plume's letter read in her own voice, and Schloss Neutaubenstein dressed as a fairy tale by moonlight)
