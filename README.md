# The Pecking Order

A cosy 3D game about a city pigeon, in one HTML file (formerly Crumb City). Play it at https://some1randomtg.github.io/crumb-city/

Fly across seven cities and a little bit of the moon, make friends, run errands and find out who really runs the pigeons.

Works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Browsers with WebGPU get the new renderer; others keep the classic look. Progress is saved in your browser.

Current version: v506 (second helpings: showgrounds in Old Town and the Harbour where the town's shows set up, a camera that turns to an event when you stop to watch it, bigger street incidents that the traffic stops for, and the Midtown and Broodlyn shops and homes filled and lit; before that v505, hot pursuit: the police redone in New Yolk and St. Petersbird, cops with their own faces and uniforms, a patrol car that pulls in at the kerb, fists and a radio search with dispatch when you get up high, a ticket in a red book when you get caught, and GOT AWAY! when you lose them; and v504, bon voyage: getting between cities, every flight into New Yolk landing its own jet, a glass front and new jet bridges at New Yolk International, the Euro Express past the vineyards and a Brussels halt with Pidge on the roof, a real Libirdy ferry crossing, and a little map of your trip while you travel)
