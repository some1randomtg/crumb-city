# The Pecking Order

A cosy 3D game about a city pigeon, in one HTML file (formerly Crumb City). Play it at https://some1randomtg.github.io/crumb-city/

Fly across seven cities and a little bit of the moon, make friends, run errands and find out who really runs the pigeons.

Works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Browsers with WebGPU get the new renderer; others keep the classic look. Progress is saved in your browser.

Current version: v409 (body language, v409: people have elbows, wrists, ankles and a neck, walk heel to toe with a bend in the knee, run with pumping arms, bring a cup to their mouth and a phone to their ear, turn their heads from the neck, and sit down onto a chair and get up again; then take a seat, v408: everyone who sits down now sits on their seat with their feet on the floor, with the chairs, benches, stools and their tables brought down to the people's height in every city, and people on the grass or a picnic blanket resting their heels on it)
