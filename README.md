# The Pecking Order

A cosy 3D game about a city pigeon, in one HTML file (formerly Crumb City). Play it at https://some1randomtg.github.io/crumb-city/

Fly across seven cities and a little bit of the moon, make friends, run errands and find out who really runs the pigeons.

Works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Browsers with WebGPU get the new renderer; others keep the classic look. Progress is saved in your browser.

Current version: v428 (breaking bread, v428: a new job for the Don before Bangcluck, a Breaking Bad parody in New Yolk: a goose lawyer on Route 66, two fennecs cooking blue rock candy in an RV at the end of Maple Grove, a police chase you drive into the city, and a ramp off Pier 7 into the belly of a blimp; then swan song, v427: King Ludwig's ball in Bavaria is a cinematic now, in by clockwork swan through his grotto, a waltz with Pidge among the mannequins to Lohengrin on a music box, Plume's letter read in her own voice, and Schloss Neutaubenstein dressed as a fairy tale by moonlight; then rewind, v426.1: a Rewind mode for playtesting, at the same address with ?rewind on the end: its own save, and a Chapters list to go back to the start of any mission)
