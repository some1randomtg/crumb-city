# The Pecking Order

A cosy 3D game about a city pigeon, in one HTML file (formerly Crumb City). Play it at https://some1randomtg.github.io/crumb-city/

Fly across seven cities and a little bit of the moon, make friends, run errands and find out who really runs the pigeons.

Works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Browsers with WebGPU get the new renderer; others keep the classic look. Progress is saved in your browser.

Current version: v507 (still waters: the water in every city reflects the boats, quays, facades and trees, and the lit windows and neon at night; boats, swans and ducklings move on it with wakes; new quays, steps, ponds and promenades along it; before that v506, second helpings: showgrounds in Old Town and the Harbour where the town's shows set up, a camera that turns to an event when you stop to watch it, bigger street incidents that the traffic stops for, and the Midtown and Broodlyn shops and homes filled and lit; and v505, hot pursuit: the police redone in New Yolk and St. Petersbird, cops with their own faces and uniforms, a patrol car that pulls in at the kerb, fists and a radio search with dispatch when you get up high, a ticket in a red book when you get caught, and GOT AWAY! when you lose them)
