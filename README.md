# The Pecking Order

A cosy 3D game about a city pigeon, in one HTML file (formerly Crumb City). Play it at https://some1randomtg.github.io/crumb-city/

Fly across seven cities and a little bit of the moon, make friends, run errands and find out who really runs the pigeons.

Works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Browsers with WebGPU get the new renderer; others keep the classic look. Progress is saved in your browser.

Current version: v488 (faces in the crowd: the people of every city have more character, with faces that show expressions, each city dressed in its own style, old folk, kids and dancers that move like themselves, busier main streets with chats on the pavements, spoked wheels and proper taxis; before that v487, room to breathe: the game needs much less memory as you travel, about 2.4 to 2.9 GB less late in the game, as the parts of cities you are not in are packed away and the graphics card lets go of cities you have left; fewer slow frames just after arriving in Paree, Tokyoo and Bangcluck; before that v486, raining cats and dogs: the animals on the ground in Pidge's own style: round, soft dogs that really walk at heel and gallop when they chase, round charm cats, Mr. Slippers and the firehouse crew walking with a swing in their tails, rabbits with faces and hopping legs, squirrels with legs, bees and moths you can see, foxes off their stilts, the raccoons in their gi on rounder bodies, and fuzzy little bats with big ears)
