# The Pecking Order

A cosy 3D game about a city pigeon, in one HTML file (formerly Crumb City). Play it at https://some1randomtg.github.io/crumb-city/

Fly across seven cities and a little bit of the moon, make friends, run errands and find out who really runs the pigeons.

Works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Browsers with WebGPU get the new renderer; others keep the classic look. Progress is saved in your browser.

Current version: v502 (family ties: the Family's jobs reworked, gang gulls you see coming on the cannoli run and Carmine counting what they left, the Admiral's beach camp with Gary's gaze drawn on the sand, the Blue Moon heist at night with alarms and a GOTCHA!, and Larry's tank packed for the road; before that v501, under the hood: St. Petersbird's seven shut doors open again, Mama Pica's front no longer pale at dusk, Talons' fitted hood, Bavaria's lake shore meeting the water, goods in every open Paree shop window; and v500, hot off the press: the city talks about you, a lit headline ticker, newsboxes with front pages, a paper in every city, passers-by who point and gossip about your deeds, and the Magpie Family's marks sharp on the shop windows)
