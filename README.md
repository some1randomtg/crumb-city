# The Pecking Order

A cosy 3D game about a city pigeon, in one HTML file (formerly Crumb City). Play it at https://some1randomtg.github.io/crumb-city/

Fly across seven cities and a little bit of the moon, make friends, run errands and find out who really runs the pigeons.

Works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Browsers with WebGPU get the new renderer; others keep the classic look. Progress is saved in your browser.

Current version: v415 (small print, v415: the open-top bus tourists sit on real seats on the upper deck with their feet on the floor, and underground riders in long coats and dresses no longer poke through the carriage floor; then clear the air, v413: the raccoons drop into the Rumble at Red Crook and get their salad bar, the wedding fire shows its flames after a skip and Notre-Dame's pews and guests are whole, the riddle checker is an 8 MB smaller download, New Yolk's streets stop steaming everywhere, the wood pigeons rest between songs and the arena crowd faces the court)
