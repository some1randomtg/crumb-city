# The Pecking Order

A cosy 3D game about a city pigeon, in one HTML file (formerly Crumb City). Play it at https://some1randomtg.github.io/crumb-city/

Fly across seven cities and a little bit of the moon, make friends, run errands and find out who really runs the pigeons.

Works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Browsers with WebGPU get the new renderer; others keep the classic look. Progress is saved in your browser.

Current version: v507.1 (elbow room: about 130 to 150 MB less memory wherever you are, nothing new to see; before that v507, still waters: the water in every city reflects the boats, quays, facades and trees, and the lit windows and neon at night; boats, swans and ducklings move on it with wakes; new quays, steps, ponds and promenades along it; and v506, second helpings: showgrounds in Old Town and the Harbour where the town's shows set up, a camera that turns to an event when you stop to watch it, bigger street incidents that the traffic stops for, and the Midtown and Broodlyn shops and homes filled and lit)
