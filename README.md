# The Pecking Order

A cosy 3D game about a city pigeon, in one HTML file (formerly Crumb City). Play it at https://some1randomtg.github.io/crumb-city/

Fly across seven cities and a little bit of the moon, make friends, run errands and find out who really runs the pigeons.

Works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Browsers with WebGPU get the new renderer; others keep the classic look. Progress is saved in your browser.

Current version: v447 (finders keepers: everything Pidge finds on the ground is the thing its name says, from a crumb that is a crumb and each city's own food to 25 shinies that were all one disc, and food hops into Pidge's bill when eaten; before that v446, odd birds: every bird that isn't a pigeon is its own kind now, from dumpling gulls and the Magpie Mob to Bangcluck's hens in eight breeds and Coco the cockatoo, each with its own call and a bill that moves when it talks; before that v444, impressionable: a painted graphic novel that starts in Orsay, six living Impressionist paintings, three choices and a painted museum ticket for Plume).
