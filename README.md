# The Pecking Order

A cosy 3D game about a city pigeon, in one HTML file (formerly Crumb City). Play it at https://some1randomtg.github.io/crumb-city/

Fly across seven cities and a little bit of the moon, make friends, run errands and find out who really runs the pigeons.

Works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Browsers with WebGPU get the new renderer; others keep the classic look. Progress is saved in your browser.

Current version: v500 (hot off the press: the city talks about you, a lit headline ticker, newsboxes with front pages, a paper in every city, passers-by who point and gossip about your deeds, and the Magpie Family's marks sharp on the shop windows; before that v499, bits and bobs: New Yolk's small games reworked, riddle plaques and roof bells, the Bridge Dash with Speedy racing you, kids' football with real goals, people who react when you ride their heads, the Gull Gang on their beach and the bagel boy's cart)
