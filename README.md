# The Pecking Order

A cosy 3D game about a city pigeon, in one HTML file (formerly Crumb City). Play it at https://some1randomtg.github.io/crumb-city/

Fly across seven cities and a little bit of the moon, make friends, run errands and find out who really runs the pigeons.

Works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Browsers with WebGPU get the new renderer; others keep the classic look. Progress is saved in your browser.

Current version: v505 (hot pursuit: the police redone in New Yolk and St. Petersbird, cops with their own faces and uniforms, a patrol car that pulls in at the kerb, fists and a radio search with dispatch when you get up high, a ticket in a red book when you get caught, and GOT AWAY! when you lose them; before that v504, bon voyage: getting between cities, every flight into New Yolk landing its own jet, a glass front and new jet bridges at New Yolk International, the Euro Express past the vineyards and a Brussels halt with Pidge on the roof, a real Libirdy ferry crossing, and a little map of your trip while you travel; and v503.1, clear view: Family Ties tidied, the cannoli box on Mama Pica's counter, a clear look at the sleeping Admiral with Gary's gaze in wider dashes, and Carmine taking Mama's call when the cannoli go soft)
