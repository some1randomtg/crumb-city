# The Pecking Order

A cosy 3D game about a city pigeon, in one HTML file (formerly Crumb City). Play it at https://some1randomtg.github.io/crumb-city/

Fly across seven cities and a little bit of the moon, make friends, run errands and find out who really runs the pigeons.

Works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Browsers with WebGPU get the new renderer; others keep the classic look. Progress is saved in your browser.

Current version: v435 (chairman of the board, v434: a new scene at the bottom of the hotel, where the lift to B5 opens on the board of the Pecking Order at its black table, to a coo choir's Dies irae and Mozart's Lacrimosa, with a photograph laid down for every chapter you have played and a choice at the end; then fun house, v435: a haunted house in Featherland, five minutes in the dark with Honkers the clown, with places to hide, doors to slam and pigeon holes he can't fit through, and his Red Nose for anyone who lasts)
