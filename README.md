# The Pecking Order

A cosy 3D game about a city pigeon, in one HTML file (formerly Crumb City). Play it at https://some1randomtg.github.io/crumb-city/

Fly across seven cities and a little bit of the moon, make friends, run errands and find out who really runs the pigeons.

Works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Browsers with WebGPU get the new renderer; others keep the classic look. Progress is saved in your browser.

Current version: v389 (bin day: Diogenes, a cockatoo who lives in a wheelie bin behind Khao Scran Road in Bangcluck, opens it the way Australia's cockatoos do and wants you out of his sunlight; his side quest, An Honest Bird, sends Pidge looking for someone who admits to telling a lie; and every city outside New Yolk runs a little lighter; then loose feathers, v388.1: rice in Bangcluck's paddies, anglers and rowing boats on the Seine by the islands, a planked Pier 9 in Tokyoo, Tour de Fromage readable on an upright phone, and the moon hotel's basement corridor lit)
