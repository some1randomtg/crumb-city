# The Pecking Order

A cosy 3D game about a city pigeon, in one HTML file (formerly Crumb City). Play it at https://some1randomtg.github.io/crumb-city/

Fly across seven cities and a little bit of the moon, make friends, run errands and find out who really runs the pigeons.

Works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Browsers with WebGPU get the new renderer; others keep the classic look. Progress is saved in your browser.

Current version: v416 (solid ground, v416: Pidge lands on hundreds more stalls, awnings, parasols, tables, ruins and roofs instead of sinking through them, and Paree and the moon arrive in a moment after a talk or a cut-scene instead of four or five seconds behind the card; then small print, v415: the open-top bus tourists sit on real seats on the upper deck with their feet on the floor, and underground riders in long coats and dresses no longer poke through the carriage floor)
