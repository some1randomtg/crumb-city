# The Pecking Order

A cosy 3D game about a city pigeon, in one HTML file (formerly Crumb City). Play it at https://some1randomtg.github.io/crumb-city/

Fly across seven cities and a little bit of the moon, make friends, run errands and find out who really runs the pigeons.

Works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Browsers with WebGPU get the new renderer; others keep the classic look. Progress is saved in your browser.

Current version: v426 (fair fight, v426: the Rumble at Red Crook is a real fight, with a crate of day-old bagels to throw at the shellfish against the clock, and its cut scenes slower and readable; the duct maze's fans have off switches and the sweepers no longer shove you into the dojo; the Feather Tree counts down properly and no longer changes how Pidge looks; a proper garden gnome fishing at Westfield Lake; then bundled up, v425: a little smoother, the most when flying over New Yolk and Paree, with nothing on screen changed; then spring clean, v424: smoother again, about 5-7% more frames a second in New Yolk, Paree, Bangcluck and St. Petersbird, people coming into view no longer show stale faces or shadows, and the game now runs on three.js 0.187, which makes arriving in a city smoother and lets the night bloom round windows and neon show)
