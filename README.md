# The Pecking Order

A cosy 3D game about a city pigeon, in one HTML file (formerly Crumb City). Play it at https://some1randomtg.github.io/crumb-city/

Fly across seven cities and a little bit of the moon, make friends, run errands and find out who really runs the pigeons.

Works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Browsers with WebGPU get the new renderer; others keep the classic look. Progress is saved in your browser.

Current version: v510 (flying the coop: New Yolk Zoo taken again, with a hippo café, a domed aviary, a penguin pool with an ice shelf and a slide and lions on a kopje; and a side quest: five zoo animals plan a break-out with Pidge as their inside bird, then live in town with a present each; before that v509, light on her feet: Pidge tips on take-off, settles into landings, nods on a peck, waddles while carrying and rolls back from a shove; the flight camera follows closer; new wingbeats, wing claps and pecks that click, knock or rustle; and v508, seconds, please: a toasted crumb, a seed pile, a bitten pizza crust and a soft shadow under every food; shinies that glint; the New Yolk hoard crate and the Bangcluck chests flip their lids open; you can see Pidge eat; and about 70 to 190 MB less memory)
