# The Pecking Order

A cosy 3D game about a city pigeon, in one HTML file (formerly Crumb City). Play it at https://some1randomtg.github.io/crumb-city/

Fly across seven cities and a little bit of the moon, make friends, run errands and find out who really runs the pigeons.

Works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Browsers with WebGPU get the new renderer; others keep the classic look. Progress is saved in your browser.

Current version: v388.1 (loose feathers: rice in Bangcluck's paddies, anglers and rowing boats on the Seine by the islands, a planked Pier 9 in Tokyoo, Tour de Fromage readable on an upright phone, and the moon hotel's basement corridor lit; then tidy nest, v388: fourteen small fixes, among them the tank parked where Larry left it after the chase, the moon rabbit asking once whether you want to pound or talk, the whole floating-market canal counting as water, and long place names that fit the clock)
